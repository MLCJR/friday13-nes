Friday the 13th (NES, 1989)

A from-scratch disassembly of the cartridge, the research written up from it, and the things built on top. The program ROM is fully mapped: 32,768 bytes, no gaps.

The Configurizerruns hereSet or reroll the cartridge: items, weapons, Jason’s route, cabin contents. Runs in your browser, and your ROM never leaves the page. Referenceon github.comThe findings, facts only, with the method left out. Includes a generated map of all 32,768 bytes of program ROM. RAM mapon github.comAll 2 KB of work RAM, byte by byte, with what each one holds and how that was established. CHR mapon github.comThe 32 KB of character ROM, both descriptor tables decoded, and the font that renders differently depending on the bank. ROM Hackson github.comWhat has been built on the disassembly so far, including a conversion that gives the cartridge the battery saves it shipped without. Patcheson github.comBPS patches, to apply to your own copy, plus what each one changes.

No ROM is distributed here, and none is uploaded anywhere. The Configurizer patches a copy you already own, in your own browser. Documents are CC BY 4.0.
